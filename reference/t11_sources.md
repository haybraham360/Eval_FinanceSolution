Workings: P1 = 5,000,000 - 400,000 - 62,500 - 200,000 = 4,337,500 chargeable, tax 330,000 + 18% of 1,337,500 = 570,750. P2 = 12,000,000 - 960,000 - 500,000 = 10,540,000, tax 330,000 + 18% of 7,540,000 = 1,687,200. P3 = 330,000 + 1,620,000 + 2,730,000 + 5,750,000 + 2,500,000 = 12,930,000. G3: 50m of 200m is not reinvested, so 25% of 5m = 1,250,000.

C3 uses a turnover of N150m on purpose. The enacted Act sets the small-company limit at N50m, while reported June 2026 guidance uses N100m, so a turnover between the two would not have one defensible answer. C4 sits at or under both limits.

G4 interaction: the employment exemption for "redundancy lump sum payment and other compensation of capital nature for loss of employment" is "subject to the provisions of Part VIII of Chapter Two", and that Part holds s.50's N50,000,000 limit, so only the excess is chargeable.
